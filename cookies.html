1 · What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website can store in your browser. They’re used to remember preferences (like your chosen theme), keep you signed in, or — in many cases — to track behavior across sites. Browser localStorage is similar but stays only on your device. SHERO uses localStorage for a single preference and uses no tracking cookies.

2 · What SHERO stores in your browser

NameTypePurposeExpires
shero-theme localStorage Remembers your light/dark mode preference Until you clear browser data

That’s it. No advertising cookies, no analytics cookies, no session-tracking cookies.

3 · Third-party services

SHERO uses Cloudflare Web Analytics for aggregated traffic data. Cloudflare Web Analytics is specifically built to be privacy-first: it does not set cookies, does not store IP addresses, and does not profile users. It collects only aggregated page-view counts. See Cloudflare’s privacy notes.

Outbound links to retailers (Shopify, Amazon, partner stores) leave our site and are governed by those sites’ cookie policies.

4 · Consent & opt-out

Because we use no tracking cookies and our analytics provider is cookie-free, EU/UK GDPR-ePrivacy and KVKK rules do not require us to display a consent banner. If we later add a service that does require consent (e.g. Meta Pixel), we’ll add a granular consent banner before that service runs.

5 · How to disable cookies

You can clear or block localStorage and cookies in your browser:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Site settings → Data stored
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Clearing shero-theme just resets the theme to your OS preference next time you visit. Nothing breaks.

6 · Changes to this policy

If we add a service that changes what’s stored in your browser, this page is updated and the “Last updated” date at the top is bumped before that service goes live.
