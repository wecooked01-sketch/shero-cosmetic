1 · Who we are

SHERO Cosmetic (“SHERO”, “we”, “us”) is a skincare brand based in Istanbul, Turkey. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal data when you visit sherocosmetic.com or interact with us by email.

For the purposes of Turkey’s KVKK (Law no. 6698) we are the veri sorumlusu (data controller). For the EU/EEA GDPR, we are the “controller” of the personal data described below.

2 · What data we collect

We collect only the minimum needed to operate the site and respond to you. We do not run an on-site checkout — orders happen on third-party retailers (Shopify, Amazon, partner stores).

CategoryExamplesSource
Contact form dataName, email, message topic, message bodyYou, when you submit the form

Aggregated traffic dataPages viewed, country, device class, referrer (no cookies, no IP)Cloudflare Web Analytics, automatically

We do not collect: payment data, government IDs, sensitive special-category data (health, religion, etc.), nor location below country level.

3 · Why we collect it · lawful bases

  • To respond to enquiries (contact form): performance of a contract / pre-contractual steps + your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)(a) · KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)).
  • To send newsletters: your consent — opt-in only, unsubscribe in every email (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) · KVKK Art. 5(1)).
  • To understand site traffic and improve content: our legitimate interest in running and improving the site, with no individual profiling (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

4 · Cookies & similar tech

SHERO uses a small number of strictly necessary preferences (light/dark theme stored in localStorage). We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not set tracking cookies, does not store IP addresses, and does not profile users. See our Cookie Policy for the full list.

5 · Who we share with

We share only what is needed to operate the site:

  • Hosting: GitHub Pages (staging) and Cloudflare Pages (production, planned).
  • Newsletter delivery: Klaviyo (planned).
  • Contact form handling: Formspree (planned).
  • Analytics: Cloudflare Web Analytics (privacy-first).

Each processor has its own DPA in place. We never sell or rent your data to third parties for marketing.

7 · Your rights (KVKK + GDPR)

You may at any time:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Ask us to delete your data (“right to be forgotten”)
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Withdraw consent for newsletters with one click in any email
  • Request a copy in a portable format
  • Complain to a supervisory authority (KVKK Kurumu in Turkey, or your local EU DPA)

To exercise any of these, email privacy@sherocosmetic.com. We respond within 30 days (15 days for KVKK requests).

8 · International transfers

Our processors (Cloudflare, Klaviyo, Formspree, GitHub) may store data outside Turkey or the EU. Transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses, adequacy decisions, or your explicit consent as applicable.

9 · Children

SHERO is for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we’ll delete it.

10 · Changes to this policy

If we make material changes we’ll update the “Last updated” date at the top and, where appropriate, notify newsletter subscribers by email. Minor edits (typo fixes, link updates) won’t reset the date.
