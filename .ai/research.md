# Research

Notes from onboarding research and any future competitive/audience work.

## Brand read (from existing copy and design)

- **Positioning:** clean, science-backed, gently luxurious — sits between
  pharmacy-clinical (The Ordinary, Paula's Choice) and lifestyle-luxury
  (Drunk Elephant, Augustinus Bader). Tone is warm and reassuring, not
  clinical, not theatrical.
- **Voice:** "Skincare made for every woman — clean, considered, and quietly
  luxurious." That word "quietly" is the key differentiator vs the louder
  end of the beauty market.
- **Visual language:** warm cream + blush + rose-gold + deep brown. Editorial
  serif (Playfair Display) for headings, geometric sans (Outfit) for body.
  Hand-drawn SVG bottles instead of photography — deliberate choice that
  reads as boutique/artisanal.
- **Hero products:** 6 SKUs (Cleanser, Essence, two Treatments, Cream, SPF)
  forming a 5-step routine. Concentrations are foregrounded (15% Vit C,
  0.3% retinol, 2% HA, 5% niacinamide) — the science-backed half of the
  positioning.

## Audience signals (from testimonial copy)

- Names suggest Turkish primary audience (Aylin, Maya, Selin, Defne)
- Age range 26–41
- Price-sensitive but not entry-level ("the price is on the higher end…
  worth every lira") — suggests mid-premium pricing tier
- "Sensitive skin and retinol always ruined me" — strong sensitive-skin
  proof point in the testimonials, aligning with the "gentle but effective"
  positioning

## Competitive landscape (high level — not deeply researched yet)

- **In Turkey:** Kuaforium, Farmasi (mass), L'Oréal Paris (mass), the
  Sephora/Watsons private labels. Premium tier is mostly imported.
- **Imported premium:** The Ordinary, Drunk Elephant, Augustinus Bader,
  Paula's Choice.
- **Direct comparables (clean-luxury, science-backed):** Tatcha, Drunk
  Elephant, Indie Lee, Saie. SHERO sits closest to early-stage Tatcha or
  Indie Lee — boutique scientific.
