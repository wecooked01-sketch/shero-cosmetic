1 · Our commitment

SHERO Cosmetic is committed to making sherocosmetic.com usable by as many people as possible, including those who rely on assistive technology. We treat accessibility as a design constraint, not a checkbox.

2 · Standards we follow

The site aims to conform to WCAG 2.1 Level AA — the same level required by the EU Web Accessibility Directive and the European Accessibility Act (EAA, in force from 28 June 2025). Where we exceed AA, we say so; where we fall short, we list the gap below.

3 · Built-in accessibility features

  • Keyboard-first navigation: every interactive element is reachable via Tab. The mobile nav drawer traps focus while open and returns focus to its trigger on close.
  • Visible focus rings on all interactive elements (:focus-visible), tuned for both light and dark mode.
  • Reduced-motion respect: if your OS is set to prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, all decorative animation is disabled and entry transitions snap to their final state.
  • Color contrast: body text meets at least 4.5:1 against background in both light and dark theme. Large display headings meet 3:1.
  • Theme toggle: a manual light/dark control persists across visits and works independently of OS-level preferences.
  • Semantic HTML: landmarks (<header>, <main>, <nav>, <footer>), descriptive headings, and labelled form fields throughout.
  • Live regions: the skin quiz announces step transitions through an aria-live region for screen-reader users.
  • Touch targets: all interactive icon-only controls are at least 44×44 px.

4 · Known limitations

The following issues are tracked and being worked on:

  • Press marquee: the magazine credit row scrolls continuously. It respects prefers-reduced-motion, but the perpetual motion may still distract some users. A click-to-pause affordance is on the backlog.
  • Decorative imagery: the inline SVG bloom in the hero is marked aria-hidden. Owner-supplied PNG decorations will get equivalent treatment when they ship.
  • Long-form legal pages: these pages haven’t yet had a manual screen-reader pass — they will before public launch.

5 · Feedback & help

If you hit an accessibility barrier or have an idea for how we can do better, please tell us. We aim to respond within five business days.

6 · Enforcement procedure

If you contact us about an accessibility issue and aren’t satisfied with our response, you can escalate to your local enforcement body:

  • Turkey: KVKK Kurumu and/or the Ministry of Trade Consumer Protection Directorate (Tüketici Hakem Heyeti).
  • EU/EEA: the national enforcement body designated under your country’s implementation of the European Accessibility Act.
