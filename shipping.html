1 · How buying SHERO works

SHERO Cosmetic is a brand site, not a checkout. Every “Shop now” CTA on this site links out to one of our retail channels — primarily our partner Shopify store and selected marketplaces. Your order, payment, shipping, and returns are handled by that retailer under their own terms and conditions.

If you ever’re not sure who fulfilled your order, the seller name appears on the receipt email and on the shipping label.

2 · Shipping

Each retailer publishes their own shipping windows, cut-off times, and rates on their site. As a general guide:

  • Turkey: typically 1–3 business days after dispatch (varies by carrier)
  • EU: typically 3–7 business days
  • Rest of world: typically 7–14 business days, customs may apply

Free shipping thresholds, duties, and tracking are determined by the retailer at checkout. The retailer’s own shipping policy is the authoritative source.

3 · Returns & exchanges

Returns and refunds are handled by the retailer where you purchased. Under EU and Turkish consumer-protection law you generally have a 14-day right of withdrawal from the day the goods arrive, with full refund of the purchase price, subject to the product being unopened (cosmetics hygiene rule).

For specific return windows, restocking fees, or to start a return, contact the retailer’s customer service team. They have the order details we don’t.

4 · Damaged or missing items

If a SHERO product arrives damaged, leaking, or missing from the order, please:

  1. Photograph the box and the affected items
  2. Contact the retailer that fulfilled the order — their support team can issue a replacement or refund
  3. If you can’t reach the retailer, email us at hello@sherocosmetic.com and we’ll help connect you
